4. Decision rule

For every record, write one row with:

  • set name;
  • title;
  • year;
  • DOI or stable identifier;
  • decision: RETAIN or DROP;
  • one-line reason.

RETAIN means the record could change the four-part gap statement. DROP means it does not. The rule follows ../../paper1/scopus_wos_search_plan.md:141-148.
