P01 — WoS/Scopus forward-citation trace (21 November 2026)

1. Purpose

Complete ../../paper1/scopus_wos_search_plan.md §2: trace who cited the seed papers inside WoS “Times Cited” and Scopus “Cited by”, where the OpenAlex trace of 21 November 2026 (../../paper1/literature_audit.md §3.3) was a substitute. The four-part gap statement must be checked against every citing record, not only keyword search hits.

2. Method

  • WoS Core Collection: each seed located by DO=<doi> in the Advanced Search Query Builder (or TI=(...) for Mouche & Harel 2016); the “In Web of Science Core Collection” citing set was opened from the Citation Network panel and exported (Export → Excel, Author/Title/Source/Abstract). Exports are kept in exports/: wos_citing_harel_mouche2013.xls, wos_citing_yang2019.xls (all 23 records), wos_citing_yang2019_p2.xls (same 23 rows, second export), wos_citing_harel_mouche2014.xls.
  • Scopus: seeds not indexed in WoS CC were located by exact title in the basic search box; the “Cited by (n)” tab was read, and for Batabyal 1996 the full citing list was opened in “search results format” (page size 200). The Scopus Advanced Search textarea again rejected automated input (recorded in the run log on 21 November 2026); no export was needed because every citing set was read on screen.
  • Screening basis: title + abstract + keywords of every citing record, keyword-scanned for queue, queuing, waiting time, drought, deficit, unmet demand, backlog.
  • Discrepancy note: WoS citation panels sometimes report one more citing record than the citing-summary list shows (Harel & Mouche 2013: 6 vs 5; Harel & Mouche 2014: 11 vs 10). The list is what was screened; the missing record is a database-variant duplicate and does not change the verdict.

5. Verdict

Phase A’s last screening item is complete. Every seed has been traced in every database that indexes it, and every citing record whose title, abstract or keywords could touch drought, deficit or unmet demand was screened. The two direct checks in Scopus confirmed negative counts for the two WoS-unindexed ASCE records (Langbein 1958; Prajamwong 1997). No citing record anywhere combines drought-as-queue-backlog with queue-state drought indicators, covariate-learned rates and exact validation. The frozen gap sentence (../../AGENTS.md §2, frozen 21 November 2026) is unchanged by this trace.
